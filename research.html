Research

I develop hybrid modelling frameworks that combine process-based models with machine learning to bridge spatial and temporal scales in land surface processes. My current work combines mechanistic insights from biogeochemistry with data-driven methods to link local observations to regional and global patterns.

I'm also interested in the intersection of complex systems, graph neural networks, and time series analysis.

Areas of focus

  • Hybrid modelling
  • Model development in Julia
  • Data Cubes
  • Handling petabyte-scale data—no memory issues!
  • Visual Analytics
  • Chaos*
